Use & support

This independent application is maintained for its owner's personal statement-processing workflow. It is not a public financial service, and this website does not offer account registration or access to the Windows application.

Authorized access

Use the application only with your own TNG account and Gmail account, or accounts whose owners have expressly authorized your access. Complete Google's authorization and any required human verification through the appropriate official interfaces.

Review the results

The application validates downloads and filtered PDFs, but external websites, email delivery, report formats, and transaction availability can change. Review files before submitting them for reimbursement. An early-month run may need a later check because recent transactions can take time to appear.

Your computer and files

Scheduled runs require a working computer, connectivity, and valid credentials. Manage local account permissions and backups. Original statements and filtered reports retain account-summary information and should be treated as personal documents.

Support

Contact the owner through GitHub Issues for general support. Do not upload statements or include passwords, OTP values, OAuth tokens, or other private information in a public issue.

See the privacy policy for details about Google data and local storage.
